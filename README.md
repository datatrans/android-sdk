Accept payments on your Android apps: Our mobile SDKs support your entire payment and registration process and simplify the integration of any payment method in your mobile apps. Completely outsource your payment processes to us from inside your native apps. We also take care of redirecting your users to 3D Secure processes and enable smooth app-switches to other payment applications including Twint and PostFinance.

## **Features**
**Easy Integration:** Integrate our payment methods into your iOS applications with just a few steps. Smart, modern and secure UI components help you deliver a seamless payment experience.

**Card Scanner**: Let your customers scan their card information with Google's native card scanner. No time wasted entering card details manually.

**Native 3DS Flows:** The SDK takes care of the complexity of the 3DS process. Whenever authentication is required, users are redirected to their bank's authorization flow and seamlessly returned to your app.

**Multiple Attempts**: Improve payment success rates by allowing users to retry when a payment fails or is cancelled. The SDK keeps the flow smooth, helping customers complete their payment without unnecessary restarts.

**Smooth App-Switch**: Do you offer payment methods like Twint or PayPal that require the user to confirm the payment in a separate mobile app? The library switches smoothly to external apps and back to the SDK.

**Saved Payment Methods and Fast Checkouts**: Save payment methods for future purchases and offer a faster checkout experience. The SDK can handle payment method selection for you.
